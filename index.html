About me

I spent 24 years at British American Tobacco leading change across supply chains, product launches and the way teams work together, in markets across Europe, Africa, the Middle East and globally. I've redesigned how organisations are structured, brought Marketing, Sales, Operations, Procurement and Finance onto the same page, and delivered savings worth tens of millions of pounds.

I now bring that experience to consulting through Crest, my own consultancy. I help organisations find the real cause of their problems, design practical solutions, and build the skills their people need to carry the change forward.

How I can help

  • Supply chain strategy

    Planning, stock levels, distribution networks and S&OP (aligning sales forecasts with what the business can supply).

  • Operating model redesign

    Clearer roles, governance and ways of working across teams and countries.

  • Faster product launches

    Getting new products from idea to shelf with less delay and less waste.

  • Cost and procurement

    Finding savings in what the business buys and how it moves goods.

  • Leading change

    Coaching-led leadership that helps new habits take root.
